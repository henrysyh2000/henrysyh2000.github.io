This entry presents an interactive visualization of one source story. LLaMA 3.1 opens by default; all five model views from the original file use the same source story and can be viewed individually or side by side.

Drag the cube to rotate it. Select a layer or token step, or click a point, to explore the linked two-dimensional trajectories and slice detail. Each model's PCA plane and ICA concept basis are fitted on all layers of its own first analogy sentence; coordinates are not a shared space across models. This single-example view does not establish a general causal mechanism.

[Open the visualization in a full window](https://henrysyh2000.github.io/assets/research-notebook/2026-10-22/concept-trajectories.html). Other experimental data, configurations, and full records remain in my private notes.
