I want to understand why each part of my engine is designed and manufactured as it is, and through which mechanisms those choices affect driving feel. These notes use a **Canadian-market 2024 Golf GTI** as their reference; other engines and market versions are identified separately.

## Identify the version first

The Canadian 2024 factory guide specifies a **turbocharged 2.0 TSI, 241 hp and 273 lb-ft**, approximately **370 N·m**. This power rating uses hp; European documents often use PS, so the numbers alone are insufficient for comparison. [Canadian 2024 factory guide, page 3](https://www.volkswagen.de/idhub/content/dam/onehub_pkw/importers/ca/2023/en/showroom/my24-general/buyers-guides/MY24_BG_GolfGTI_EN_Oct18.pdf)

Read the name in four layers: **EA888 is the family; evo4 / Gen 4 is the technical generation; GTI is the application; Canadian 2024 specifies the market and model year.** The exact engine code still needs checking against the vehicle's records. “EA888” alone cannot identify every component and calibration.

The SSP's GTI table lists **1,984 cm³** displacement, an **82.5 mm** bore, a **92.8 mm** stroke and **four valves per cylinder**. These are reference specifications from the North American training document, not the dimensions used to draw the illustration. [SSP 822223, printed page 1](https://content.datarunners.net/content/SSP/822223.pdf#page=4)

## Locate the mechanisms in factory illustrations

Printed page 5 of Volkswagen SSP 822223 illustrates the piston, rod and crankshaft assembly; page 6 introduces the timing drive. Check each illustration's application: output classes, markets and revisions can use different components. No model claiming to reconstruct production dimensions or internal oil passages is supplied here.

## Where the EA888 came from

**2006: the family begins.** Audi SSP 436 traces the launch of the chain-driven 1.8-litre TFSI, initially installed transversely in the Audi A3. Transverse, longitudinal and emissions variants developed over time. Early documents use development-stage terminology; there was no single date when every vehicle worldwide changed generation. [Audi SSP 436, PDF page 2](https://device.report/m/09635552556337959384aab480addff21151050605d130384dacf2f1906d2eda.pdf#page=2)

**2013: the Mk7 GTI explicitly uses generation three.** Volkswagen's release discusses efficiency, emissions and the head-integrated exhaust passages. Generation-three development already extended beyond output alone. [Volkswagen's 2013 GTI technical introduction](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/new-golf-gti-international-press-presentation-2976)

**2020: the Mk8 GTI uses evo4.** The European launch version produced 245 PS, matching the previous GTI Performance's peak rating, while changes addressed injection, internal friction, acoustics and emissions. Equal peak power does not establish identical internal design. [Volkswagen's 2020 engine introduction](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/the-new-golf-gti-international-media-drive-6282/engine-of-the-new-golf-gti-6285)

**2024: markets still need separating.** The European facelift release describes a 265 PS evo4 GTI. That is not the official rating of the Canadian 2024 GTI. [Volkswagen's European facelift information](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/the-new-golf-gti-and-golf-gti-clubsport-18404/the-legendary-gti-turbocharged-engine-in-two-output-levels-18408)
