Today I focused on the quality term in set-level evaluation. When an output's value is combined with how much it differs from outputs already selected, what determines that value? The local trials completed today pointed in different directions and made me more cautious about interpreting them.

This entry is a qualitative summary. Complete experimental records and original examples remain in my private notes.

## From differences within a set to the quality term

Today's work adapted a set-level evaluation and tested it on a limited scope. Each candidate's contribution depends on a quality term and on how much difference it adds relative to outputs already selected. The combination itself does not establish which analogies deserve to be rewarded.

I used a quality construction and semantic distance adapted to the analogy task. Retaining a way of combining these terms does not preserve its measurement meaning from another task, nor does it make the result a validated measure of creativity.

## A trial using structural complexity as a quality proxy

In the completed pilot, I used complexity of linguistic structure and information organization as the quality proxy. Hybrid had higher complexity-weighted set utility than the greedy baseline. This describes set performance under that proxy. Structural complexity does not automatically imply a more apt relationship, better expression, or greater creativity.

The evaluation setup and scope differed from yesterday's broader complexity analysis, so their records remain separate. Today's result cannot be relabeled as a new conclusion from that earlier analysis. A result favoring hybrid also does not justify renaming complexity as quality.

An independent model-assisted review of the rating rationales found that some omitted recoverable embedding, temporal relations, or causal dependencies. Others treated emotion, imagery, or accumulated description as structural layering. Method labels were visible during this review. It was not a blinded human evaluation and did not produce gold judgments that could simply replace the original ratings. The development material remains exploratory; verifiable calculation records do not establish a valid scale.

## Adding a fixed positive reference

I then added a fixed positive reference for each source story, using the dataset's officially correct option. References were used for evaluation after generation and were not supplied to the generator. Each method faced the same reference. Pairwise judgments exchanged the positions of candidate and reference, and the resulting relative preference became the quality term combined with set distance.

In this completed local comparison, hybrid's reference-relative preference and corresponding set utility remained below greedy's. That observation needs to stay in the record. It does not establish a full cross-model ranking, and it cannot be combined with the complexity trial on a common scale. The trials differed in scope and evaluation basis, so their different directions cannot be attributed directly to a single factor.

## What a reference can constrain

A positive reference supplies a workable analogy. It neither exhausts the correct answers nor sets a ceiling on human creativity. Relative evaluator preference is not accuracy. Exchanging positions helps examine position effects, but does not establish that all biases have been removed.

Close reading of individual examples left mixed judgments today. Some expressions moved to a fresher situation; others shifted the relationship in the source story. Observing that a reference is conventional does not by itself establish that a generation method is more creative overall. Unusual expression still needs to be examined for the relationship it preserves; unfamiliarity alone is insufficient.

## What the day's work established

At the close of the day's records, generation coverage in the main study had progressed, with some groups completed. The last verified snapshot still showed generation as incomplete, while unified cross-model evaluation and the complete report were not ready. Neither a successful job nor a historical error state can establish that all work is finished, much less that a research hypothesis holds.

Today clarified how the definition of the quality term determines what a set evaluation rewards. Completing a local calculation, placing a method ahead, and establishing a credible measurement meaning each require their own checks. Earlier quality trials remain separately archived. Comparisons that did not establish an advantage, and doubts about the evaluation scale, remain part of the record too.
