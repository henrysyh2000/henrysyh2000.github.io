Make the World My Major!

This is an adaptation of NYUSH's motto "Make the world your major" (or "让世界成为你的课堂" in Chinese). Having spent four wonderful years there, this motto has constantly motivated me to explore more cities and landscapes, dive into their cultures, and learn from these experiences worldwide.

I tend to wander into the everyday parts of a city rather than collect its landmarks. The timeline below follows the small details, quiet corners, and unplanned encounters that made me stop and look twice.
